# Current Problems presented by the System.
- Confirmed Problems
- Indirect Problems
- Possible Problems (unconfirmed)

## Problems:
- Availability issues, for both students and faculty.
- Staff's time is taken up by inefficient system design.
- Ease of contact/use of services.
- Confidentiality issues.
- Unclear information at the disposal of students.

## Indirect Issues
- Staff may create conflicting timetables due to multiple methods of booking appointments.
- Students may not get appropriate supports due to lack of information.
- It is likely a Student would fall in-between the cracks due to the over-complexity and unreliability of the current system.
- Students may book appointment that they do not need due to unclear information, resulting in staff and students time being wasted.
- Leaking of private information.
- Students who require immediate support may not be capable of getting it due to a backlog in an inefficient system.

## Possible Issues
- Lack of resources
- Lack of time
- lack of staff
- Overlapping bookings
- Meetings being pushed back due to unforeseen changes, resulting in issues not being solved in time.
