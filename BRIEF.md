# Scenario B — College Student Support Appointment System 

A college provides a range of student support services, such as academic support, 
careers advice, learning support and other student services. 

At present, students may contact services by email or phone, while staff use calendars, 
spreadsheets and other informal methods to manage appointments. 
The college wants to investigate a software system that could make appointment 
management easier. 

## Current problems include: 
- students may be unsure which service they should contact  
- students may not know when appointments are available  
- staff spend time managing appointments manually  
- cancellations and changes can be difficult to manage  
- different staff may have different appointment types and availability  
- information about students and appointments must be appropriately protected  
- accessibility and ease of use need to be considered as the Requirements Engineer, investigate questions such as:
  - Who can make an appointment?  
  - Who can manage appointment availability?  
  - What types of appointments are available?  
  - What information is required when making an appointment?  
  - Can students cancel or change appointments?  
  - What happens when a student does not attend?  
  - What reminders should be provided?
  - Who should be able to see appointment information?  
  - What privacy and security requirements apply?  
  - What accessibility requirements should be considered?
