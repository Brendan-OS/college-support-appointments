## Solution Must:
- Have a clear path to arrange an appiontment, confusing instructions, advice and aderts should not be put out.
- Clear windows where appiontments can be made, If there are exceptions they should be treated as such and all affected parties should be notified if they are affected.
- Staff time is important, the wasteage caused by the chosen soltuon should minimise how much work they manually do. System cannot be entirly automated without human oversight, at the very least.
- There must be a clear and decisive polocy on cancelations and rearrangments for appiontment times, each person organsing a meeting cannot have their own personal & indepentant policy.
- Different types of appiontments (Appiontments that have different requirements to the norm. such as longer time or a specailised meeting location) needs to be organised as such. this information should be readily avaiable to affected parties.
- Privacy must be kept for all stakeholders.

The solution choosen must be easily implemented either instead of, or over the current system. This is to ensure it is used and used correctly by Stakeholders, especailly those who previously operated under the pervious system.

### Other Potential Requirments
It's likley that further appiontments or meetings will need to be made in years to come, the solution should consider potential problems or issues the future may pose and organise accordingly.
The most likley method would be to make sure the choosen solution does not directly or otherwie interfear with any foreseeable potential meetings in the future.
