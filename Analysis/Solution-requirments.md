## Solution Must:
- Have a clear path to arrange an appointment, confusing instructions, advice and adverts should not be put out.
- Clear windows where appointments can be made, If there are exceptions they should be treated as such and all affected parties should be notified if they are affected.
- Staff time is important, the wastage caused by the chosen solution should minimize how much work they manually do. System cannot be entirely automated without human oversight, at the very least.
- There must be a clear and decisive policy on cancelations and rearrangements for appointment times, each person organizing a meeting cannot have their own personal & independent policy.
- Different types of appointments (Appointments that have different requirements to the norm. such as longer time or a specialized meeting location) needs to be organized as such. this information should be readily available to affected parties.
- Privacy must be kept for all stakeholders.

The solution chosen must be easily implemented either instead of, or over the current system. This is to ensure it is used and used correctly by Stakeholders, especially those who previously operated under the pervious system.

### Other Potential Requirements
It's likely that further appointments or meetings will need to be made in years to come, the solution should consider potential problems or issues the future may pose and organize accordingly.
The most likely method would be to make sure the chosen solution does not directly or otherwise interfere with any foreseeable potential meetings in the future.
