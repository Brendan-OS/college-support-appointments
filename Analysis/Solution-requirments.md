## Solution Must:
- Have a clear path to arrange an appointment, all Staff should be capable of advising students on how to go about arranging an appointment - The method(s) of doing so could be included in any pre-existing workshops that operate to ensure student understanding of college systems.
- Clear windows where appointments can be made, During the workweek and during the times the college is open would be an expected default, however depending on scheduling and the timetable of the stakeholders involved in the appointment - arrangements could be made on a person to personas bases.
- Staff time is important, the wastage caused by the chosen solution should minimize their workload. System cannot be entirely automated without some human oversight, at the very least. The Solution should aim to provide clear information to involved staff to avoid repeating work & allow an easier time gathering needed information.
- There must be a clear and decisive policy on cancelations and rearrangements for appointment times, each person organizing a meeting cannot have their own personal & independent policy. This must be arranged with the relevant stakeholders before initialization. 
- Allowance for different types of appointments, (Appointments that have different requirements to the norm. such as longer meeting times or specialized meeting locations) which would need to be organized as such. This information should be readily available to affected parties.
- The system must be capable of keeping sensitive documentation and information confidential, only the parties explicitly given consent from the persons may be privy to view & use this type of info. If an additional party requires any sensitive information to complete a task or duty, the persons who the information belongs should be asked and notified of any changes and/or updates.


### Other Potential Requirements
It's likely that further appointments or meetings will need to be made in years to come, the solution should consider potential problems or issues the future may pose and organize accordingly.
The method would make sure the chosen solution does not directly or otherwise interfere with any foreseeable potential meetings.

The System should be capable of up-or-down scaling it's operations in reference to the student/staff body. Further the system should not disallow the creation of new appointment types that may not currently be in consideration. (Such as off-campus meetings).
