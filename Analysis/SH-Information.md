The Student body should be provided a survey to discover how the system could be arranged in such a way to benefit them. Staff should also be given a similar survey but with the questions more specific to their wants/needs since they will be the most likely to schedule the appiontments.
Due to Staff being a considerably smaller population than students, an effort to arrange interviews with them should be considered to find out any possible concerns or wants they may have that are not/cannot be covered in the survey. Most certainly any & all staff currently involved in the current system of booking/arranging appointment should be interviewed. 

### Potential Survey Questions;
- How students currently arrange appointments?
- What difficulties do students experience while creating said appointments?
- What information do students need before booking an appointment?
- What improvements do students think would be useful?

- Would Staff be willing to accommodate meetings outside of the regular college hours?
- IF NO; why?
- What resources would Staff need to make said meetings more useful for students?
- How would Staff handle cancelations and/or rescheduling requests?
- How are appointments currently recorded and tracked?
- Which parts of the current process take the most time?
