## Current situation
The Current situation of the Appointment Booking system is one of inefficiency.
"At present, students may contact services by email or phone, while staff use calendars, 
spreadsheets and other informal methods to manage appointments."
From the brief it can be deduced that the staff are currently preforming most of the workload manually. 
While students are not being given clear guidance on how to contact Support Service Staff should they wish to create an appointment. 

## Identified problems
- Students may not know which service to contact.
- Students may not know appointment availability.
- Staff spend time managing appointments manually.
- Cancellations and changes can be difficult.
- Different staff may have different appointment types and availability.
- Student and appointment information needs protection.

Some problems may not be ones that are currently issued by the given brief but will still be considered during each stage of the solutions  development

## Effects of these problems
- Manual appointment management may be resulting in additional administrative workload for staff.
- Students may avoid booking appointments for support when they are in need due to ignorance, the awkwardness of it or concerns about confidential matters.

## Unknowns requiring investigation
- What are the frequency of appointments missed and/or rescheduled?
- Do students currently receive reminders about appointments? (automated or otherwise)
- How are appointment cancellations currently handled?
- Can different support services follow different procedures?
- What information do staff need from students when arranging/to arrange an appointment?
- Who currently has access to student and appointment information?
- FROM ABOVE: How is that access controlled?
