## What Questions need/should/could be asked?
### Core Questions;
- Who should students make contact with to arrange an appointment?
- Who will receive appointment/meeting requests?
- How will timetable changes be handled?
- Who would have permission to reschedule appointments? 
- How will Stakeholders privacy be kept?
- What information are students expected to provide when arranging an appiontment?


### Important Questions;
- How are appointments Scheduled & communicated to relevant parties?
- What will be the policy on cancelations? (preferably this should be a single policy to cover most cases - with exceptions made for exceptional situations)
- What support services could these appointments help with? what could they provide?
- How will information be chosen to be sensitive or not?
- If information is chosen to be sensitive, how will it be kept confidential?

### Extra Questions;
- Is there a criteria that must be met before someone can arrange an appointment?
- Will reminders be provided?
- What accessibility access should be considered for the stakeholders involved? (such as online meetings)
- Will there be repercussions and/or follow-ups if a stakeholder does not, show up for the scheduled time and/or refuses to arrange their end of the booking.
- Can appointments be arranged on behalf of others? Such as a keyworker arranging a meeting with the DSS Officer on behalf of a student within the Special Support Service.
- What are, if any, restrictions on appointment times, duration & location?
- Should an unforeseen event occur (power outage, black ice, red-storm warning) what is the procedure? will there be a back-up system to continue operations as normal? (recomended)


## Which Stakeholders can Help Answer These Questions?
### Core Answers;
- Appointment Arrangements: Students/Receptionist(s)/Meeting Persons
- Meeting Requests: Receptionists/Meeting Persons/Management
- Timetable Changes: Receptionists/Meeting Persons/Management
- Reschedule Permissions: Receptionists/Meeting Persons/Management
- Privacy: DPO/Management/IT Technicians
- Student Information: Students/Receptionists/Meeting Persons/Support Staff

### Important Answers;
- Schedule Communications: Receptionists/Support Staff/Management/Meeting Persons
- What Services: Support Staff/Students/Meeting Persons/Management
- Sensitive or non: DPO/Management/Students
- IF YES; How: DPO/IT Technicians/Meeting Persons

### Extra Answers;
- Meeting criteria: Meeting Persons/Receptionist/Management/Support Staff
- Reminders: Students/Receptionist/Management
- Accessibility: Students/Meeting Persons/Management/IT Technicians
- Repercussions: Management/Students
- Meetings made by others: Meeting Persons/Students/Management/Outside party (i.e. Keyworker)
- Meeting Restrictions: students/Meeting persons/management/Cleaning & Facility Staff
- Unforeseen Events: Management/Students/Meeting persons. IT Technicians/Receptionist
