## What Questions need/should/could be asked?
### Core Questions;
- Who should students make contact with to arrange an appointment?
- Who will receive appointment/meeting requests?
- How will timetable changes be handled?
- How will Stakeholders privacy be kept?

### Important Questions;
- How are appointments Scheduled?
- What will be the policy on cancelations?
- What can these appointments help with?
- Is the information for these appointments going to be publicly and readily available? 

### Extra Questions;
- Is there a criteria that must be met before someone can arrange an appointment?
- Will reminders be provided?
- What accessibility access should be considered for the stakeholders involved?
